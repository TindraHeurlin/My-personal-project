# THE CHESS CLIMB  
*by Tindra Heurlin*

A chess-themed vertical bouncing game where you control a chess piece climbing an endless chessboard.  
Jump between platforms, collect star powerups and rise through the ranks from Pawn to Queen as the speed increases over time.

---

## How to Play
- Use **arrow keys** (left, right, down) to move across the chessboard.  
- The player **automatically bounces** on platforms.  
- Collect **star power-ups** to gain points and upgrade your chess rank.  
- Don’t fall off the screen — that ends the game!

---

## Features
- Chess-themed design and elegant color palette (black, white, brown, red, and gold).  
- Infinite vertical scrolling and smooth camera follow.  
- Sound effects for bounce, collect and game over.  
- Background classical music for tension, but still matches the elegant theme.  
- “How to Play” panel, score and rank UI and Game Over screen.

---
